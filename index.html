about me

I am a passionate full stack web developer with a solid foundation in both frontend and backend development. I've completed intensive full-stack training at New Horizons Nigeria, graduated from the HNG internship programme, completed a Frontend Development Internship at Decodelabs, and currently work as a Junior Developer at Acclon Summit while pursuing a B.Sc. in Information Technology at Redeemer's University, Nigeria.

My journey in tech started with curiosity and a determination to create impactful web solutions. Through my comprehensive training at New Horizons, I built a strong foundation in modern web technologies and development practices, and the HNG internship sharpened those skills through real collaboration with developers across Africa. At Decodelabs, I worked with Vue.js, Tailwind CSS and JavaScript on real client projects, gaining hands-on exposure to production-level frontend development, and I'm now building on that experience as a Junior Developer at Acclon Summit. Alongside this, I've built and deployed several freelance websites for clients, including a full portfolio site for an Operations & Executive Partner. I bring fresh perspectives, quick learning abilities, and unwavering enthusiasm to every project, with a particular interest in creating efficient, user-friendly web applications that solve real-world problems.
